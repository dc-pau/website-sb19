This is SB19

The Kings of PPOP

SB19 is a Filipino P-pop boy group formed in 2016 under ShowBT Entertainment and now managed by their own company, 1Z Entertainment. They debuted in 2018 with the single Tilaluha and quickly rose to fame with Go Up in 2019. Known as the trailblazers of Pinoy Pop (P-pop), SB19 became the first Southeast Asian act nominated at the Billboard Music Awards (Top Social Artist), proving their global reach. Their official fandom is called A’TIN, symbolizing the group’s bond with fans: “SB19 and A’TIN are always together.”
